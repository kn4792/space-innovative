About

Space Innovative was founded in 2005 and brings together Pakistan’s most accomplished designers and construction management.

The portfolio includes the Boeing 747 Park in Karachi, the ISI Headquarters in Islamabad, and corporate fit-outs for global names including BAT, Philip Morris and PepsiCo.
