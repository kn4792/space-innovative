Boeing 747 Park

A landmark public park built around a decommissioned PIA Boeing 747 aircraft. Space Innovative designed and constructed all landscaping, structural steel works, and an immersive interior restaurant experience inside the aircraft — featuring luxury seating, custom lighting, and bespoke bar counters.
