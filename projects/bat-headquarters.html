BAT Headquarters

A high-end interior architecture and construction project for the British American Tobacco (BAT) headquarters in Lahore. Space Innovative delivered a comprehensive workspace solution featuring modern open-plan offices, a vibrant cafeteria with custom circular lighting, and a sophisticated executive lobby. The design integrates sleek architectural finishes with functional ergonomic zones to reflect a global corporate standard.
